How the price is worked out

1. Work: hours × your hourly rate. 2. Revisions: revision hours × your rate, so changes are paid for. 3. Expenses and contingency: added on top. 4. Profit: a true margin — it is a % of the final price, not just added on top. Example: cost $2,420 with 20% margin = $3,025 price, profit $605. 5. Platform fee: this is the important part — the fee is added on top, not taken off. Example: to keep $1,000 after a 10% fee, you must quote $1,111.11, because the platform then takes $111.11 and you keep $1,000.

PDF: press “Print / Save as PDF”, then choose “Save as PDF” in your browser. No extra file or internet tool is needed, so it works every time.

What is in the quotation?

Your total price, the full breakdown, the deposit to start (50%), how many revision rounds are included, what is not included, and that the quote is valid for 14 days. Print it, or choose “Save as PDF” in your browser, and send it to your client.

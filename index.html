      How the price is worked out

      1. Work: total hours × your hourly rate. 2. Revisions: rounds × hours per round × rate. Example: 2 rounds × 2 hours × $50 = $200. 3. Expenses and contingency: added on top. 4. Profit margin vs markup: margin is a % of the selling price. Costs $1,000 with 20% margin = $1,250 price, $250 profit. A 20% markup would give $1,200, only $200 profit — 16.7% of the price, not 20%. 5. Platform fee: the fee is added on top of the pre-tax price, so you keep your price after the fee. Example: costs $1,200, margin 20%, fee 10% = $1,666.67 price, $166.67 fee, you keep $1,500, profit $300. 6. Tax: added last, on the price after the fee. Tax is not profit.
